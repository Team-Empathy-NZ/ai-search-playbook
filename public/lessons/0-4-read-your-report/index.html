Read your report in detail

8 min read·Baseline

You've got a report. Now read it like a strategist would: check what's measured and what's estimated, find the one number that matters most, and turn the five actions into your plan for the modules ahead.

Before you read: measured or estimated?

Every number in your report should be labelled. Measured means it came straight from your data, like Search Console impressions or GA4 revenue. Directional or modelled means Claude estimated it.

Both are useful, but they carry different weight. Make decisions on the measured numbers and treat the estimates as a sense of scale. If a number has no label, ask Claude where it came from before you lean on it.

1. The executive summary

Three things to know, then the scenario table: Today, Conservative, Mid and High. Look at the gap between Today and Conservative first. Conservative is the version you should plan around. High is what it looks like if everything goes right, so don't build your budget on it.

2. How AI-ready you are today

Your score out of 100 across the four pillars. Put it next to the score you gave yourself in 0.2. If they're miles apart, find out why: usually you've been generous on a pillar, or Claude couldn't see something you know about.

Each pillar points to part of this playbook:

  • Technical is Module 1.
  • Content is Module 2.
  • Authority is Module 3.
  • Measurement is Module 4.

Your lowest pillar is the module that will pay you back fastest. 80 is AI-ready, and that's the target.

3. The market you're missing

This is the section most people skim, and it's the one that matters most. Check the branded vs non-branded split. Branded searches are people who already know you, and no strategy grows them much. Non-branded is everyone shopping your category who hasn't found you yet. That's the market you can win.

Look at which non-branded searches you already appear for, and where you rank. Searches where you're sitting on page two are the closest wins in the whole report.

4. The opportunity, in dollars

Three numbers to find:

  • The value of a non-branded click. What one visitor from a category search is worth to you.
  • Your share of the market today. How much of the winnable pool you already hold.
  • The value of each extra 1% of share. This is the number to remember. It tells you what the work is worth before you start it.

Sanity-check it. Your branded revenue should sit on top, untouched. Any share above 25% should be flagged as a stretch. If the dollar columns are marked directional, the report should tell you which piece of data would firm them up, so go and get it.

5. What to do next

Five actions in priority order. Match each one to the lesson that covers it: blocked AI crawlers is 1.1, missing product schema is 1.4, empty collection pages is 1.5, no content answering buyer questions is Module 2, not enough reviews or mentions is Module 3. That list, in that order, is your path through the playbook.

Pressure-test it

Claude can be wrong, so check two things yourself before you act. Open yourstore.com/robots.txt and confirm what it said about the AI crawlers. Then ask ChatGPT two or three of the buyer questions from the AI visibility test and see whether the answers match. If something doesn't line up, tell Claude and ask it to rework that section.

Do this now

Read the report section by section. Write down four things: your score out of 100, your lowest pillar, the value of an extra 1% of market share, and the five actions in order. Then run the prompt below in the same Claude chat to turn them into your plan.
